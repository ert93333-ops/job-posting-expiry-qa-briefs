JobPosting structured data checklist

Use this checklist before publishing job detail pages, ATS mirrors, remote job pages, job boards, and expired job cleanup updates. It is designed for pasted-sample QA when crawling, Search Console, Rich Results Test, and ATS APIs are unavailable.

Audit sample JobPosting markup

1. Required JobPosting fields

  • Confirm the page has one JobPosting for one job detail page.
  • Confirm `title`, `description`, `datePosted`, `hiringOrganization`, `identifier`, and location handling are present.
  • Confirm the job is still open before leaving markup live.

2. validThrough and expiry

  • Confirm `validThrough` is present where the job has a close date.
  • Flag roles that are filled, paused, closed, or no longer accepting applicants.
  • Record who removes or updates expired JobPosting markup.

3. Page type

  • Use JobPosting markup on a single job detail page, not a search, list, category, or archive page.
  • Confirm canonical and sitemap handling for duplicate ATS mirrors.
  • Confirm only the intended job is represented by the schema.

4. Visible/schema parity

  • Compare visible title, description, department, location, and status against schema.
  • Flag stale roles, renamed roles, and locations that differ from the ATS source.
  • Confirm applicant-facing content matches the structured data.

5. Remote and location handoff

  • For remote jobs, confirm `jobLocationType` and applicant location requirements are intentional.
  • For physical jobs, confirm address country and location details are assigned.
  • Record who owns hybrid, remote, and office-location cleanup.

6. Apply URL and directApply

  • Confirm the apply URL is current, public, and accepting applicants.
  • Do not claim directApply unless the applicant flow is actually direct.
  • Flag login walls, stale ATS requisitions, redirects, and closed apply links.

7. Salary, benefits, and owners

  • Review salary/benefit fields against visible page copy and owner policy.
  • Name recruiting, ATS, frontend, content, and SEO owners for each finding.
  • Add a fix path and retest date before handoff.
