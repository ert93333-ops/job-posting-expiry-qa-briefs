FAQ

JobPosting QA constraints are explicit.

Does it crawl career pages?

No. It produces a launch QA brief from pasted metadata and notes only.

Does it call Search Console or Rich Results Test?

No. This MVP is browser-local and does not call Search Console, Rich Results Test, ATS platforms, or external APIs.

Does it connect to an ATS?

No. ATS status and apply URL details are pasted as notes for static QA only.

Does it provide hiring or legal advice?

No. It is technical SEO and launch QA guidance, not legal, HR, hiring, compliance, ranking, indexing, or applicant-flow advice.
